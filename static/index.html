Rules

  1. Use the arrow keys or WASD keys to move the snake and eat the food,lookout out for hidden advancements.

  2. Each time the snake eats food, it grows longer by 1 cell, its health increases.

  3. Food Health Immunity
    Apple +1
    Pumpkin pie +4
    Golden Carrot +5 5s
    Golden Apple +5 7s
    Enchanted Golden Apple +10 10s

  4. Walls are instant death
    They stop you if immune. (take a different direction!).

  5. Bumping into your own body is an instant death, but you will pass through if you have immunity.

  6. Lava is instant death, unless you have immunity.

  7. Rotten flesh will decrease your health by 1 for 5 seconds unless you have immunity.

  8. Magma decreases your health by 0.5 for every contact unless you have immunity.

  9. Soul Sand,Blue Ice give a speed buff of 0.5x,2x respectively.
  10. Final Score Status
    < 20 Coughing Baby
    20-100 Hydrogen Baby
    > 100 Hydrogen Bomb

GAME OVER

Press ENTER to restart game
Press H to go to homescreen
